## Keybinds

* Command (ou Cmd) ⌘
* Maj ⇧
* Option (or Alt) ⌥
* Contrôle (or Ctrl) ⌃

## Hidden files

How to unhide .folder names ?

> ⇧ + ⌘ + .

## Path

Bring the path to the buttom of your finder window

> ⌥ + ⌘ + P

## Go

How to go to specified directory

> ⌥ + ⌘ + G
